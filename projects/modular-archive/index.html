Modular Archive

A working study of modular publishing, spatial rhythm, and deliberately incomplete grids.

The archive is treated as a spatial index rather than a conventional portfolio. Every object keeps its own scale while remaining part of one chronological stream.

One system, several densities

Text follows a readable sequence. Images can temporarily enter a four-column media grid when their relationship matters.

The empty cells on the homepage are not repaired. They reveal the rules of the system and keep chronological order intact.
