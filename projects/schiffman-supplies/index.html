Schiffman Supplies

A visual identity built around connections, movement and a modular structure.

A system for moving things

Schiffman Supplies works with industrial manufacturers and suppliers in the B2B sector. The project brief brings together supply chains, import and export, distribution and consulting — activities that connect different parts of a larger system.

The visual identity translates those relationships into a modular language: an open geometric frame, connecting lines and a smaller, coloured element held inside it.

Different connections. One recognisable structure.

From mark to visual language

The frame can turn, repeat and become a pattern. Colour introduces variation without replacing the underlying geometry. The presentation extends the same vocabulary to supplies, logistics, management and consulting.

Across scales

The proposed applications move from packaging to transport and signage. The same structure appears as a small signature, a repeated pattern or a large mark on an industrial surface.
