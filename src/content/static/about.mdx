I'm Alexey Farber. A designer and artist working with brands.

</TextBlock>

I bring a project's scattered information into focus: what it stands for, how it works and what makes it distinct. That understanding becomes the starting point for its visual identity.

<TextBlock align="center" variant="large-italic">

Character, from the inside out.

</TextBlock>

<TextBlock align="right">

Research, brand architecture and artistic interpretation are parts of the same practice. I work from the internal logic of a project towards a visual language that can grow without losing its character.

</TextBlock>

Commercial projects, art and writing live here together. They are different ways of exploring how ideas take form — under one name, FARBER.
